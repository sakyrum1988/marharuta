# Editorial review: cost-of-living-in-thailand (EN)

## Scope and audience

- Reader task: estimate a sustainable Thailand budget and separate monthly living costs from relocation and annual expenses.
- Audience: international long-stay residents, remote workers and retirees; no nationality is assumed.
- Language and search market: English, Google US.
- Research date: 2026-10-29.
- Format: new practical cost guide based on the competitor URL in the editorial queue.
- Status: **Ready for editorial review; not published**.

## Competitor review

| Page | Strength | Gap addressed in our article |
|---|---|---|
| Expatra, Cost of Living in Thailand | Broad overview of rent, food and city differences | The competitor URL ranks for a tight 15-key cluster, but a single average does not show first-month cash or currency risk |
| Life Here, Cost of Living Thailand 2026 | Clear city and lifestyle budget matrix | We explain the assumptions behind the ranges and separate official tariffs from market estimates |
| Thailand Insider Guide, Real Monthly Budgets | Extensive household and city ranges | We avoid false precision and show how to rebuild the budget from the chosen neighbourhood |
| Expatica, Cost of Living in Thailand | Accessible category-by-category overview | We add a first-month formula and explicit exclusions |
| Numbeo Thailand and city pages | Current city rent and price references | Crowdsourced data is labelled as a reference, not presented as an official average |

Additional value: city-specific planning ranges, official electricity and water inputs, exchange-rate date, first-month cash requirements and a clear boundary between official tariffs and market estimates.
