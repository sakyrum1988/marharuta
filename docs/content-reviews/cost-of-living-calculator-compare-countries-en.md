# Editorial review: cost-of-living-calculator-compare-countries (EN)

## Scope and audience

- Reader task: use a cost-of-living calculator without mistaking a general index for a personal relocation budget.
- Audience: English-language readers comparing an international move; household, citizenship, income source and tax residence are not assumed.
- Language and search market: English, Google US.
- Research date: 2026-11-04.
- Competitor URL: `https://getwherenext.com/blog/cost-of-living-calculator-compare-countries`.
- Format: calculator-use and verification guide, not a cheapest-country ranking.
- Status: **Ready in local development**.

## Competitor review

| Page | Strength | Gap addressed |
|---|---|---|
| WhereNext, Cost of Living Calculator: Compare Any Two Countries | Clear category breakdown, action sequence and visibility of hidden expenses | The page presents many precise country and city figures with broad source labels; our guide avoids repeating unverified numbers and teaches the reader to audit data date, basket and applicability |
| Numbeo, Methodology and Motivation | Detailed disclosure of crowdsourcing, filters, recency rules, basket weights and NYC reference | A transparent general index is still not a personal budget; our article translates the methodology into household-level checks |

Additional value: the article separates screening from verification, distinguishes national PPP context from city and household budgets, explains why CPI cannot compare price levels between cities, and converts one monthly result into normal-month, annualised, arrival-spend and cash-required layers.
