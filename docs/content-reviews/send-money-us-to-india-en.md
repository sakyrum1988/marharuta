# Editorial review: send-money-us-to-india (EN)

- Research date: 2026-11-08
- Search database: Google US (`g_us`)
- Audience: people in the United States sending a personal remittance to a recipient in India; citizenship is not assumed.
- Search intent: compare compliant ways to send a personal transfer and choose by the recipient's final INR, delivery method, timing, and sender protections.
- Author experience: none claimed. The article does not present third-party experience as first-hand experience.
