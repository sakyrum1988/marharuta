# Editorial review: digital-nomad-visa-countries (EN)

## Scope and audience

- Reader task: turn a global list of digital-nomad routes into a legally workable shortlist.
- Audience: English-speaking international remote employees, freelancers and company owners; no nationality is assumed.
- Language and search market: English, Google US.
- Research date: 2026-11-02.
- Competitor URL: `https://citizenremote.com/blog/digital-nomad-visa-countries/`.
- Format: global comparison framework with representative official programmes, not a static exhaustive list.
- Status: **Ready in local development**.

## Competitor review

| Page | Strength | Gap addressed |
|---|---|---|
| Citizen Remote, 59 Digital Nomad Visa Countries | Broad discovery list with recent update and country sections | Large count hides programme type, evidence fit and maintenance cost; country data ages quickly |
| Nomad Hub, Digital Nomad Visa Index | Clear filters, original currencies, review dates and source methodology | Twenty-country index still requires the reader to convert data into a personal shortlist |
| Visa365, Digital nomad visas | Concise country summaries with official links and passport framing | Strong for discovery, lighter on employer approval, tax triage and full-year cost |
| Visa Decision, Countries With Digital Nomad Visas | Useful “catch” column and separation of non-nomad visitor routes | Comparison remains data-dense and does not provide a repeatable applicant scorecard |
| IBA, The Digital Nomad Report | Professional cross-country evidence on application location, duration and renewability | Survey is not a consumer decision guide and does not replace current official programme pages |

Additional value: six elimination filters, a three-country shortlist process, an evidence scorecard, total-route budgeting and five official examples across the Americas, Asia and Europe. The article explicitly separates dedicated status from visitor permission and avoids claiming an exhaustive country count.
