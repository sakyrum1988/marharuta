- **Audience:** remote workers considering Japan who need to know whether the six-month status can support a longer stay
- **Research date:** 29 October 2026
- **Status:** editorial, technical and visual QA passed locally; ready for commit/publication, not pushed

## Search intent and editorial decision

The page must answer the extension question immediately, then distinguish extension, temporary re-entry and a later new application. The strongest broader demand is for the Japan digital nomad visa itself, so the page also covers the requirements that change the decision: the six-month limit, foreign work only, JPY 10 million income threshold, insurance, lack of a residence card and the six-month interval before the status may be used again.

The previous version answered the headline question but was too short to support a complete decision. It also treated the interval before reapplication as uncertain even though the current Immigration Services Agency Q&A answers it directly.
