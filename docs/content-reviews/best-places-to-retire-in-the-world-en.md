## Scope

- Reader task: compare practical international retirement destinations without confusing a lifestyle ranking with legal residence eligibility.
- Audience: international retirees, including Americans considering retirement abroad; no citizenship is assumed.
- Search market: Google US.
- Research date: 2026-10-29.
- Status: **Ready for editorial review**. Responsive rendering and required checks were completed on 2026-10-29.

## Corrected competitor-led intent

The source URL is `https://internationalliving.com/the-best-places-to-retire/`. Its Serpstat URL report contained 1,298 ranking phrases. The earlier Asia-only cluster was incorrect because it came from a separate seed-keyword report rather than the competitor URL. The corrected Google Sheet cluster contains 38 phrases selected directly from the URL export.

Primary intent: identify and compare the best countries, cities and international destinations for retirement abroad.

Secondary intents: best options for Americans, overseas residence routes, healthcare access, proof of income, capital requirements and destination trade-offs.

Separate intents not forced into this article: cheapest retirement destinations, Europe-only rankings, Asia-only rankings, domestic US retirement and tax-only comparisons.

## Editorial angle

Choose the residence route and healthcare geography before choosing the postcard destination. The article separates monthly spending, first-year costs and capital locked in deposits or property. It also explains why consular thresholds cannot be copied across jurisdictions.
