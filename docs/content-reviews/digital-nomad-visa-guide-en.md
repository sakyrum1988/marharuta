# Editorial review: digital-nomad-visa-guide (EN)

## Scope and audience

- Reader task: understand what a digital nomad visa permits, decide whether one fits, and prepare an application file.
- Audience: English-speaking international remote employees, freelancers and foreign-business owners; no nationality is assumed.
- Language and search market: English, Google US.
- Research date: 2026-11-02.
- Competitor URL from the queue: `https://citizenremote.com/blog/digital-nomad-visas-what-are-they-and-how-do-i-get-one/`.
- Format: definition, route comparison and application guide.
- Status: **Ready in local dev; not published to production**.

## Competitor review

| Page | Strength | Gap addressed |
|---|---|---|
| Citizen Remote, Digital Nomad Visas: What are they and how do I get one? | Accessible definition, benefits, requirements and broad country coverage | Broad overview does not sharply separate immigration permission, local work, employer approval and tax; country lists age quickly |
| Fragomen, Digital Nomad Visas Explained | Professional overview of common eligibility and documents | Generic programme summary; does not give a reader a document-consistency workflow |
| Genki World, Digital Nomad Visa Comparison | Large comparison with tax and insurance prompts | Volume of country data obscures the first decision and carries an insurance-sales context |
| EY, Global Immigration Index for Digital Nomads | Strong employer-compliance framing and cross-country criteria | Index is useful for policy comparison, not a step-by-step applicant workflow |
| Residency Malta, Nomad Residence Permit | Current official eligibility and application checklist | One-country source; cannot be generalized to other programmes |

Additional value: separates immigration status, permitted activity, employer approval and tax; uses three current official examples to show material differences; provides an eight-step evidence workflow and explains when another route fits better.
