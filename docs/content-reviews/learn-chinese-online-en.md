# Editorial review: learn-chinese-online (EN)

## Scope

- Reader question: How can a beginner learn Chinese online without wasting money or building a fragmented study routine?
- Audience: English-speaking adults of unspecified citizenship; people preparing for travel, relocation, everyday conversation or HSK. No prior Chinese level is assumed.
- Search region and language: Google US, English.
- Research date: 2026-11-16.
- Editorial angle: a goal-first system and a four-week trial, not a timeless ranking of commercial apps.
- Author experience boundary: no personal Chinese-study experience is attributed to Margarita Yarovenko. Recommendations are editorial conclusions based on competitor review and the cited primary/provider pages.
