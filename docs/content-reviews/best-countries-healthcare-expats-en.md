# Editorial review: best-countries-healthcare-expats (EN)

## Scope and audience

- Reader task: shortlist a country by both healthcare-system quality and the reader's actual access as a foreign resident.
- Audience: English-speaking prospective expats; no citizenship, diagnosis or immigration status is assumed.
- Language and search market: English, Google US.
- Research date: 2026-10-31.
- Queued competitor: `https://internationalliving.com/countries-best-healthcare-world/`.
- Format: decision comparison, not a universal ranking.
- Status: **Ready in local dev; not published to production**.

## Competitor review

| Page | Strength | Gap addressed |
|---|---|---|
| International Living, *5 Best Healthcare Systems for Expats in 2026* | Country examples for France, Portugal, Spain, Panama and Costa Rica; useful lived-experience questions | Personal prices and anecdotes are not reused as current facts; the article separates system quality from legal entitlement |
| MoveBuddha, *Countries with the Best Healthcare for Expats* | Fast comparison of cost, access and popular destinations | Broad claims about legal residents and taxes are replaced with country-specific official conditions |
| InterNations, *Quality of Life 2026* healthcare results | Current expat-perception data and a transparent survey period | Perception is not presented as clinical outcomes or proof of eligibility |
| OECD, *Health at a Glance 2025* | Comparable dimensions for access, quality, outcomes, spending and resources | OECD dimensions become a comparison framework rather than a single winner |
| WHO, Universal Health Coverage | Authoritative definition using service coverage and financial hardship | Explains why “universal” or “free” does not mean automatic full coverage for every foreign resident |

Additional value: a five-question eligibility framework, official access rules for five countries, an explanation of the arrival-to-enrolment gap, and a pre-move insurance checklist. Panama and Costa Rica are not carried into the shortlist because current official entitlement evidence was not established to the same standard during this review.
