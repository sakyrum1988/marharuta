# Editorial review: is-vietnam-safe-to-live (EN)

## Scope

- Reader question: Is Vietnam safe enough for long-term living, and which risks should an expat test before choosing a neighbourhood?
- Audience: English-speaking adults considering relocation; citizenship, city, gender and health needs are not assumed.
- Search region and language: Google US, English.
- Research date: 2026-11-16.
- Editorial angle: distinguish low violent-crime exposure from daily friction caused by traffic, theft, scams, weather, health care and law; add a seven-day neighbourhood test.
- Author experience boundary: no personal experience of Margarita Yarovenko is claimed. Practical recommendations are editorial conclusions based on the sources below.
