# Editorial review: best-places-to-live-in-thailand (EN)

## Scope

- Reader question: Which Thai city or island is the most workable long-term base for an expat?
- Audience: English-speaking prospective expats of unspecified citizenship; remote workers, professionals, families, couples and retirees are separated by scenario.
- Search region and language: Google US, English.
- Research date: 2026-11-10.
- Editorial angle: a decision framework for ordinary weekly life, not a universal ranking. The article compares transport, healthcare access, air quality, community, climate and reversibility.
- Author experience boundary: no personal Thailand experience is attributed to Margarita Yarovenko. Recommendations are editorial conclusions based on the cited research.
