# Editorial review: cheapest-places-to-live-abroad (EN)

## Scope and audience

- Reader task: shortlist inexpensive overseas bases without confusing a monthly lifestyle estimate with the full cost of relocation.
- Audience: international solo renters, remote workers and early retirees; no nationality is assumed.
- Language and search market: English, Google US.
- Research date: 2026-10-29.
- Status: **Ready for editorial review; not published**.

## Competitor review

| Page | Strength | Gap addressed |
|---|---|---|
| International Living, Cheapest Places to Live in the World in 2026 | Five destination narratives and lived-budget examples | Personal budgets are not standardised and legal stay is secondary; our article compares one basket and adds status/setup filters |
| Settled Nomad Cost Index 2026 | Comparable city basket with explicit inclusions and exclusions | We add immigration, first-month cash and reader-fit decisions |
| Expatriates.ai cost-of-living comparison | Broad country ranges connected to visa pages | We narrow to five Asian bases and distinguish travel permission from residence/work rights |
| Numbeo 2026 Mid-Year Country Index | Broad current crowdsourced price signal | We do not present the index as an official budget or a city quote |
| Kiplinger, $100-a-Day Retirement | Explains lifestyle assumptions and the local-versus-imported consumption gap | We provide a reproducible shortlist and startup-cost checklist |

Additional value: a like-for-like monthly signal, explicit exclusions, legal-stay screening, first-month cash test and a boundary between short visits and residence.
