# Editorial review: what-is-an-expat (EN)

## Scope and audience

- Reader task: understand what “expat” means, how it overlaps with immigrant, migrant and digital nomad, and which practical questions the label cannot answer.
- Audience: international readers considering or already living abroad; no nationality is assumed.
- Language and search market: English, Google US.
- Research date: 2026-10-30.
- Format: new terminology and relocation-planning guide based on the competitor URL in the editorial queue.
- Status: **Ready in the local dev blog; not published to production**.

## Competitor review

| Page | Strength | Gap addressed in our article |
|---|---|---|
| International Living, What Is an Expat? | Accessible definition, lifestyle examples and move-abroad encouragement | Romanticises the identity and does not clearly separate the label from visas, work permission and tax status |
| Expatica, The Difference Between Expats and Immigrants | Direct discussion of class, race and unequal usage | Limited practical guidance for a reader preparing a move |
| Remitly, Expat Meaning in the UAE | Clear term comparison and regional context | UAE-specific and sometimes presents a cleaner distinction than real usage supports |
| World Bank, The Power of Words | Explains how privilege shapes the expat/migrant distinction | Commentary rather than a relocation decision guide |
| Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary | Concise, neutral common-language definition | Does not explain legal limits or planning consequences |

Additional value: one table comparing five overlapping terms, explicit separation of social identity from legal status, a practical status checklist, balanced treatment of the word’s class and race associations, and a concrete move-abroad sequence.

## Existing-site overlap

The site uses “expat” throughout country, visa and cost pages but has no dedicated article defining the term or explaining its limits. This page supports those existing guides and links readers to the visa guide, planning guide, comparison tool and budget planner instead of duplicating country-specific rules.

## Fact register

| Claim | Source | Checked | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Expat is an informal term for a person living in a country that is not their own | Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary | 2026-10-30 | Common-language definition, not a legal category |
| There is no formal universal legal definition of international migrant; UN statistical usage focuses on change of usual residence | United Nations Refugees and Migrants definitions | 2026-10-30 | Statistical framing differs from national immigration law |
| IOM migration terminology covers many distinct categories and contexts | IOM Glossary on Migration 2019 | 2026-10-30 | A reference framework, not a substitute for national law |
