# Editorial review: safety (EN)

- Audience: English-speaking people comparing Asian countries and cities for relocation; no citizenship assumed.
- Research date: 2026-10-26. Competitor market: live English-language search. Keyword database: Serpstat Google.ru, used as supporting evidence because no Google US export was available.
- Reader task: assess personal relocation safety without treating one ranking as a complete answer.

## Competitors and editorial gap

Reviewed Numbeo Safety Index, International Living's safest places coverage, InterNations' safety guide, Remitly's Immigration Index and WhereNext's Asian rankings. Competitors either emphasise a composite score or general precautions. This page adds six separate risk checks, city/neighbourhood verification and an emergency plan.

## Keyword evidence

Serpstat returned 72 related Russian-market phrases for the seed “безопасные страны”. Relevant intents included safest countries, safety rankings, safest countries to live in and safety for women. Difficulty was available (1–5 for the selected terms); frequency was unavailable and is not recorded as zero. English phrasing is based on the same intent, not claimed English volume.
