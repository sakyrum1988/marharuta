## Competitor research

Five live English-language pages were reviewed:

1. DTV Club, “Thailand Long-Stay Visa Comparison”. Strong side-by-side route comparison; commercial DTV context and limited treatment of the September 2026 short-entry change.
2. Anglo Siam Legal, “DTV vs Elite vs Retirement Visa”. Useful profile-led comparison; legal-service framing and less separation between visa validity and stay per entry.
3. Paglipat, “Thailand DTV Compared”. Clear DTV positioning; narrower coverage of tourist entry, LTR and current passport-specific rules.
4. Bangkok LAD, “Thailand Long-Stay Visas Compared”. Broad option set; limited primary-source evidence beside changing claims.
5. Asoke Legal, “DTV vs Elite vs LTR”. Useful route distinctions; lead-generation emphasis and little help with a pre-payment evidence workflow.

The article’s added value is one decision path across short entry, tourist visa, DTV, LTR, retirement and Thailand Privilege; explicit separation of visa validity, permitted stay and work permission; the September 2026 entry change; and direct official sources.
