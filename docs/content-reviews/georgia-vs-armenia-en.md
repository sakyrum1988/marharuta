## Scope

- Reader question: Should I choose Georgia or Armenia for a first trip, a combined route or a trial move?
- Audience: English-speaking independent travellers and prospective movers of unspecified citizenship.
- Search region and language: Google US, English.
- Research date: 2026-11-06.
- Editorial angle: a like-for-like decision framework that separates tourism from relocation and avoids turning one traveller's prices into national averages.
- Author experience boundary: no first-person experience in Georgia or Armenia is attributed to Margarita Yarovenko. The recommendations are editorial conclusions based on current sources.

## Editorial decisions

- The supplied 63.ru article is treated as an anecdotal competitor, not evidence of national costs, safety or language ability.
- Exact accommodation and meal prices were omitted because a current, equivalent basket for both cities was not available from primary sources.
- Russian and English usage is described as situational; no population is assigned a language preference.
- Tourism, visitor status, work permission and residence are kept separate.
- The URL contains no year, as required.
