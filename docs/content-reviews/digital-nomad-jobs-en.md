# Editorial review: Digital Nomad Jobs

## Scope

- Slug: `digital-nomad-jobs`
- Language: English
- Audience: international job seekers who want remote work that can be performed while living or travelling abroad; no citizenship is assumed.
- Search market intended for research: Google US (`g_us`).
- Research date: 2026-11-08.
- Reader task: distinguish a genuinely location-flexible role from a home-based remote vacancy, find suitable openings, prepare evidence of remote readiness and verify an offer before travelling.
- Editorial angle: a five-gate vacancy filter and a measurable 30-day search funnel, not a generic list of online professions.

## Intent overlap

The local database contains articles about the digital nomad lifestyle and digital nomad visas, but no article dedicated to finding and assessing remote work. The new page links to those existing decision pages instead of repeating their visa or lifestyle coverage.
