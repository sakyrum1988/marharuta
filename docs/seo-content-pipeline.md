# SEO Content Pipeline

This is the working process for new and refreshed articles on Relocate to Asia.

## 1. Context Trigger

Start with the keyword, page intent and a short editorial angle. The angle must make the article different from a generic AI summary.

Examples:

- "Japan Digital Nomad Visa" is not only a visa guide. The angle is the six-month limit and why it is not a relocation route.
- "Cost Of Living Asia" is not a cheap-country list. The angle is full relocation budgeting: rent, visa rhythm, insurance, deposits and exit risk.

## 2. Research

Collect:

- official rule sources;
- current SERP headings and competitor subtopics;
- low-intent and long-tail questions;
- gaps competitors miss;
- numbers that need verification.

Store the working notes before drafting. Do not invent visa exceptions, renewal options or budget certainty.

## 3. Internal Content

Before writing, check existing pages for:

- relevant country guides;
- visa guides;
- comparison pages;
- tools that help the reader decide;
- old claims that need updating.

Every article should naturally connect to the next decision page.

## 4. Outline

Use BLUF:

- answer first;
- explain what the rule says;
- explain what it means in practice;
- show trade-offs;
- explain who fits and who does not;
- finish with a decision, not a generic conclusion.

Sections should be mutually exclusive. No repeated paragraphs with swapped country names.

## 5. Product / Tool Annotation

Add internal tool mentions only where they help the decision:

- Cost Calculator for budget uncertainty;
- Budget Planner for first-month relocation costs;
- Compare Countries for country-vs-country decisions;
- country guides for deeper planning.

Do not force tool mentions into legal or factual sections.

## 6. Drafting

Write like a practitioner explaining the issue to another person:

- direct opening;
- varied sentence rhythm;
- no filler intros;
- no "in this article";
- separate fact from interpretation;
- clear risk language.

Russian pages are localized, not mechanically translated.

## 7. Verify Claims

For every factual claim:

- check the official source;
- keep external official links nofollow;
- mark uncertain or changing rules clearly;
- avoid unsupported claims about extensions, dependants, work permission or hidden paths.

If a fact cannot be verified, remove it or soften it.

## 8. Preview

Before publishing:

- render the local page;
- check one H1;
- check title and meta description;
- check source panel;
- check mobile layout;
- check internal links;
- run `python scripts\seo_audit.py`.

## 9. Refresh Old Content

When updating old pages:

- find outdated statistics and rule claims;
- add missing official sources;
- compare current SERP subtopics;
- add relevant internal links;
- expand thin sections with practical decision detail.
