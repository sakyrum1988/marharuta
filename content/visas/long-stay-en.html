Long-stay visas in Asia: compare the real route

A long validity does not reveal how long each stay lasts, what work is permitted or whether the status leads to residence. Define the scenario first, then compare programmes.

What “five years” may actually mean

It can mean a five-year visa with 180 days per entry, an annual permission renewed repeatedly, a status with minimum-presence rules, or a programme renewed while a deposit remains. Record validity, stay per entry, extension, reporting, re-entry and maximum total stay as separate fields.

Then verify activity. Some programmes expressly cover overseas remote work; others are designed for retirement or lifestyle and do not grant local work rights. The label “long stay” is not work permission.

A practical shortlist

Thailand

DTV, LTR and retirement routes solve different problems. Compare savings, income, insurance, stay pattern and work rights.

Malaysia

DE Rantau and MM2H are not substitutes: one focuses on remote professionals, the other on financial conditions for long residence.

Taiwan, Japan and Korea

Professional and nomad routes can be strong but may be limited by income, nationality, occupation or duration.

Indonesia and the Philippines

Remote, second-home and retirement scenarios exist; verify a live application channel, not only a programme announcement.

Verification plan

Open the authority's portal, select nationality and filing location, and save the current checklist. Convert currencies with headroom rather than holding exactly the threshold. Compare both the first year and renewal: medical certificates, document legalisation, insurance, deposits and travel can make an apparently cheap programme expensive.

Common questions

Is a long-stay visa a residence permit?

Not necessarily. It may describe only visa validity or permitted stay. Check the legal status on the authority's site.

May I work?

Only when the programme or a separate permission expressly covers the intended activity.

Which programme is easiest?

The one where real income, age, work and family circumstances meet the rules without aggressive interpretation.
