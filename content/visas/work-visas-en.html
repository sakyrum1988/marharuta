Work visas in Asia: compare the route before accepting an offer

A work visa usually starts with a role, an employer and a permission category. Compare the right to perform the actual job and the consequences of changing sponsors, not the programme name.

Quick answer

Singapore and Hong Kong suit strong international profiles; Japan and South Korea reward candidates with a defined occupation and employer; Taiwan has separate talent routes. In Thailand and Malaysia, local employment must be separated from remote work. For most standard categories the employer applies or formally supports the application.

Seven tests for a work route

  1. Sponsor: who applies, and does the employer need a quota or licence?
  2. Occupation: is the job restricted to local workers?
  3. Salary: does the threshold change with age, sector or qualifications?
  4. Evidence: which degrees, references and years of experience must be proved?
  5. Family: can dependants move, and may a spouse work?
  6. Job change: is a new approval required before the first day?
  7. Termination: how long remains to change sponsor or leave?

Do not pay for a “guaranteed offer” or hand an original passport to an intermediary without a lawful reason. Verify the company in an official registry and retain copies of every submission.

Career markets are different choices, not a ranking

Singapore and Hong Kong

Strong for finance, technology, trade and regional headquarters. Seniority, salary and evidence of specialist value are central filters.

Japan and South Korea

Large markets with occupation-based routes. Language, qualification recognition and local hiring practice may matter more than the visa form.

Taiwan and Malaysia

Useful for specialists and regional teams; compare the standard employment pass with independent talent or remote routes.

Before filing

  1. Find the authority's exact category.
  2. Match each requirement to a document.
  3. Model net income after housing, tax, insurance and transport.
  4. Plan for delay and refusal.
  5. Do not resign or sign a long lease before the decision is clear.

Common questions

Can I enter as a visitor and start work?

Visitor admission normally does not authorise local employment. Permission should be valid before work begins.

Can I change employer?

Often, but a fresh filing or approval may be required. Do not begin the new role on an old sponsor's permission without an official check.

Does a work visa lead to permanent residence?

Some residence years may count, but permanent-residence tests are separate and can include continuity, income, tax, language and integration.
