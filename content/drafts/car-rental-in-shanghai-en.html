Car rental in Shanghai is possible for foreign visitors, but a foreign driving licence or an International Driving Permit is not enough on its own. To drive legally, a short-stay visitor normally needs a temporary driving permit issued by the Chinese traffic police; a longer-term resident may instead convert an overseas licence or obtain a regular Chinese licence. The rental branch must also agree to accept your passport, permit, payment method and route.

This distinction matters: government permission to drive does not guarantee that a particular company will release a car, and a paid booking does not give you permission to drive. Confirm both parts before choosing a non-refundable rate.

Should you rent a car in Shanghai at all?

For a trip contained within central Shanghai, a car is often more work than help. Metro, taxis and ride-hailing remove the need to obtain a permit, collect a vehicle, find parking and deal with an unfamiliar road system. A rental becomes more useful when the itinerary includes several countryside stops that are awkward to connect by rail and local transport.

The two approvals you need

The process has two separate gates:

  1. Legal authority to drive. The Shanghai traffic police issue the temporary permit. For rental vehicles it covers only small cars in classes C1 or C2, and the class must match the entitlement on the foreign licence.
  2. Acceptance by the rental branch. The company decides whether it will accept the document type, passport, driver’s age, payment card, additional drivers and planned return location. Its commercial rules may be stricter than the legal minimum.

Ask the branch to confirm acceptance in writing before prepaying. Send only the minimum information needed at that stage; do not transmit full passport or card images through an unverified chat account.

Temporary driving permit: documents and process

Shanghai’s official guidance says visiting foreigners can apply for a temporary driving permit. Prepare the original passport or other entry-and-exit identity document and a valid overseas driving licence. If the licence is not in Chinese, obtain a Chinese translation accepted by the authority.

The applicant submits the documents at a vehicle management office, has a photograph taken and completes the required road-law learning session. Shanghai also provides an online application route through the Traffic Management Corps platform, but the official instructions still describe in-person steps before the permit is issued.

  • Applicants for a small car or small automatic car must be at least 18.
  • A stay of less than three months can support a three-month temporary permit.
  • For a stay longer than three months, the permit can be valid for up to one year.
  • Applicants aged 70 or over must pass the specified capacity test.
  • Unresolved traffic cases, disqualifying records or incomplete and mismatched documents can prevent issuance.

The issued document—not a blog or booking confirmation—controls the exact validity and vehicle class. A temporary driving permit does not extend the holder’s immigration stay.

What to carry while driving

Shanghai’s traffic-police guidance says the temporary permit must be used together with the overseas driving licence and its Chinese translation. When driving a rental vehicle, carry the rental contract as well. Keep the originals accessible rather than relying only on phone photographs.

The official Shanghai FAQ also says a temporary permit may be used in other provinces and cities. That does not override the rental agreement: separately confirm the permitted route, one-way return, mileage, cross-province assistance and any areas excluded by the company.

Shanghai car rental checks before payment

Rental prices and deposit policies change by date, vehicle, channel and branch, so a single advertised number is not a reliable budget. Request a written quote that separates the base rate from taxes or service charges, insurance and excess, deposit or pre-authorisation, additional-driver fees, one-way fees, fuel or charging policy, toll handling and late-return rules.

Confirm these points before choosing a restrictive booking:

  • the exact branch accepts a foreign passport and your temporary or regular Chinese driving credential;
  • the named payment card can be used for both the rental and the security hold;
  • every person who may drive is registered and accepted;
  • the vehicle category matches the permit, passenger count and luggage;
  • the planned provinces, return city and mileage are allowed;
  • the insurance exclusions and maximum amount payable after damage are stated clearly;
  • the process for traffic notices received after return is explained.

Pickup and return checklist

At pickup, compare the vehicle registration and plate with the contract. Photograph every side of the car, the wheels, glass, cabin, dashboard, mileage and fuel or battery level. Make sure existing damage is recorded before accepting the keys.

Ask how to contact roadside assistance and what to do after a collision, breakdown or tyre problem. Do not promise a private cash settlement after an accident without following the contract and local reporting procedure. At return, photograph the vehicle again, obtain a return record and keep the contract until the deposit and any later traffic-violation process are settled.

Temporary permit or regular Chinese licence?

A temporary permit is designed for a short stay and a limited class of vehicle. Someone living in Shanghai longer may prefer to convert a valid overseas licence. The official city guide describes document submission, a medical check and a written traffic-law test for conversion; applying from scratch follows a longer training and examination route.

Frequently asked questions

Can I drive a rental car with my foreign licence?

Not with the foreign licence alone. For a short visit, Shanghai’s official route is a temporary driving permit based on the overseas licence and an accepted Chinese translation.

Is an International Driving Permit enough?

No. Shanghai’s official guidance requires the locally issued temporary permit. The foreign licence and its Chinese translation support the application and must be carried with the permit.

Can I take the rental car outside Shanghai?

The city’s official FAQ says the temporary permit allows driving in other provinces and cities. The rental contract may still limit the route or return location, so obtain the company’s written confirmation.

Is car hire in Shanghai useful for a city break?

Usually not for a central-city itinerary. It makes more sense when several stops cannot be connected efficiently by train and local transport, and the permit and booking requirements are already resolved.
